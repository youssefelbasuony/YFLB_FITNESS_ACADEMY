# YFLB Fitness Academy — visual direction

## Product
Beginner gym equipment learning and workout guidance. The UI is used before and between sets, often on a phone. Primary tasks are find a machine, save it to this gym, and understand one movement.

## Pattern and visual style
Use an athletic editorial interface with clear content hierarchy and a restrained, high contrast treatment. Favor strong typographic scale, deliberate alignment, generous page margins, and a few purposeful blocks over a dashboard full of cards. Use sharp or lightly rounded corners; avoid gradients as decoration, faux profile content, and gamified stats.

## Colors
- Light background: warm chalk `#F3F2EE`; surface: `#FFFFFF`; text: ink `#191A18`; muted text: `#62645E`; border: `#DEDED8`.
- Dark background: graphite `#171918`; surface: `#222522`; text: `#F4F3EE`; muted text: `#B0B1AA`; border: `#3A3D39`.
- Brand action: signal orange `#F15B3B`, with dark ink text where contrast permits; use for the main action, selected state, and key wayfinding.
- Maintain visible text contrast in both themes. Black logo on light; white logo on dark.

## Typography
Barlow Condensed for display headings and Barlow for body/UI. Use a system sans-serif fallback. Keep large headings tight and athletic; body copy readable at 14px or larger where practical.

## Interaction
Make the first useful action visible on initial load. Keep search and machine category filters close together. A saved machine gets a clear text label and selected state, not color alone. Use 44px minimum touch targets. Keep motion short and honor `prefers-reduced-motion`.

## Responsive
Desktop: compact sidebar or horizontal rail with content focused in a centered readable column. Mobile: concise top bar and bottom navigation, one column, full width search, horizontally scrollable category chips, and touch-friendly cards.

## Avoid
Generic stock dashboard stats, decorative fake accounts, multiple competing CTAs, emoji icons, tiny text, excessive cards, low contrast gray, and machine guidance that looks like professional coaching claims.
