# UI and design system

## Direction

YFLB is an Arabic-first beginner guide for use on a gym floor. The opening screen should answer three questions quickly: What does this site do? What should I do first? Where do I find the guide for my machine? Show real app content in the home preview, then lead straight into searchable equipment.

## Tokens and typography

- Warm neutral canvas, white surfaces, forest-green structure, and lime action color.
- Dark mode uses graphite-green surfaces and readable off-white type.
- Use the supplied black logo on light surfaces and white logo on dark surfaces; each logo links to Home.
- Alexandria is the Arabic UI and reading font. Manrope is used for English and Latin text.
- Arabic copy uses short, contemporary pan-Arabic wording. Avoid Egyptian slang, unexplained gym terms, and decorative English labels in Arabic screens.

## Interaction hierarchy

1. Find a machine or muscle in the member's gym.
2. Open one guide and follow setup, movement steps, and safety cue.
3. Learn the target muscles and related machines.
4. Save equipment after signing in, then revisit it from the member's list.
5. Follow a starter session when ready.

Use direct labels and visible next steps. Do not force a tutorial, show fictional progress, or imply an account is needed just to browse. Keep touch targets at least 44px, forms labeled, keyboard focus visible, dialogs accessible, and reduced motion respected. Use responsive desktop navigation and mobile bottom navigation.
