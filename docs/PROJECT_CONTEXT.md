# Project context — YFLB Fitness Academy

## Vision

Help people feel capable in the gym from their first visit through their first year of training. Reduce confusion with clear, practical education grounded in the equipment a member can actually use.

## Audience

New and early-stage gym members, especially people who feel intimidated, overloaded by conflicting social media advice, or unsure how equipment works. The interface launches in Arabic with an English toggle; explain terms rather than assuming gym experience.

## Core experience

1. Members choose the machines available at their gym.
2. Their chosen equipment is attached to their account and easy to find again.
3. Each guide explains setup, movement steps, muscles involved, and a practical cue; the YFLB admin can attach an instructor video.
4. Selecting a muscle explains its basic role and points to relevant equipment.
5. A manageable starter plan gives members a place to begin and records completed sessions.

## Product principles

- Make the next action obvious.
- Teach without shaming or overwhelming.
- Use short cues that can be remembered on a gym floor.
- Keep the interface phone-friendly and bilingual.
- Review exercise and safety content with a qualified professional before public use.
- Keep member data private and make server-side authorization the source of truth.

## Brand

Product name: YFLB Fitness Academy. Use the provided black logo on light backgrounds and white logo on dark backgrounds. The visual direction is confident, fresh, and practical, with forest green, bright lime, and high contrast.

## Source boundary

The archived Night Gym Academy files and inherited documents are historical context. Current user direction and current YFLB documents define active scope.
