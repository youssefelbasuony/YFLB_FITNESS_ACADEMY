# Beginner gym companion

## Product goal

Help people feel capable at the gym from their first visit through their first year. The product starts with the equipment in a member's actual gym and answers practical questions at the point of use.

## Current experience

- Arabic-first interface with English toggle and light and dark themes.
- Home introduces a simple three-step way to get started.
- Academy offers searchable, categorized machine guides.
- Members can save machines to an account and find them again in the “My gym” filter.
- A machine guide explains setup, movement steps, target muscles, what the member may feel, and one cue to watch. Admins can attach local instructor videos.
- Muscle explanations link back to related equipment.
- A three-session sample week supports a gentle first routine; signed-in members can mark sessions complete.
- About explains why the academy is designed for beginners.

## Working principles

1. Give the member a useful next action instead of a dense dashboard.
2. Keep guidance brief enough to check between sets.
3. Prefer the actual gym's equipment and clear technique cues over trend-driven advice.
4. Do not claim sample movement or routine content is professionally reviewed before it is.
5. Keep the mobile flow clear and every important interaction keyboard accessible.

## Current limits

Local account email and password sign-in works without an email provider. Password recovery and verification delivery are not set up. Local SQLite and filesystem video uploads need durable production replacements for many deployment platforms. Sample exercise content still requires qualified coach review.
