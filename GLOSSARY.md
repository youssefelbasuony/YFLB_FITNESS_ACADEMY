# YFLB Fitness Academy glossary

- **Academy:** The learning library for gym equipment, movement basics, and muscles.
- **Machine guide:** A short guide to a piece of gym equipment, including setup, steps, muscles, coaching cues, and an instructor video when available.
- **Member:** A person with a YFLB account. Machine selections, fitness profiles, workout schedules, set logs, and completed sessions are stored per member.
- **My Gym:** The member's saved list of machines available at their gym.
- **Muscle group:** A beginner-facing anatomy topic connected to relevant machine guides.
- **Training plan:** A translated workout template with a goal, experience level, weekly session count, split days, prescriptions, coaching rationale, and evidence reference. The owner can publish or unpublish it.
- **Training split:** The arrangement of plan days and the muscle groups or movement patterns emphasized on each day.
- **Fitness profile:** A member's goal, experience level, gym familiarity, preferred weekdays, available session time, equipment, language, and time zone.
- **Workout session:** A dated instance of a plan day, saved with a content snapshot so later plan edits do not rewrite past or assigned sessions.
- **Set log:** A member's recorded load and repetitions for an exercise set in a session.
- **Rescheduling:** Moving overdue planned sessions onto the member's next selected training weekdays while preserving completed work and saved logs.
- **Localized media:** A machine's video or animation uploaded for a specific interface language. A different language never receives the English recording as a fallback.
- **Admin:** The single trusted YFLB owner who can manage translated machine guides, localized demonstrations, and training plans. Local development lets the first signed-in account claim ownership; production requires the configured `ADMIN_EMAIL` and private `ADMIN_ACCESS_KEY`.
- **Settings:** The personal preferences menu for light/dark theme, interface language, five text-size levels, account access, and the admin entry point.
- **Prototype content:** Sample equipment and exercise guidance that requires qualified review before public production use.
